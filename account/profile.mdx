## Manage your account from one page

Your account page brings together the profile details you can update, the account details you can review, and shortcuts to the areas you are likely to need next.

If you are signed in, open `/account` to view your profile, filing and payment counts, API key access, and logout controls. If you are not signed in, Nomadfiling redirects you to `/login` before showing the page.

<Callout kind="info">

The account page is available only after authentication. If Nomadfiling sends you to the login page, sign in first and then return to `/account`.

</Callout>

## What you can do on the account page

The account page is organized around a few core account tasks:

- Review your saved profile information
- Update your full name inline
- Check how many filings you have created
- Check how many paid payments are on your account
- Open your MCP API keys page
- Sign out of your account

Behind the scenes, the page loads your profile details and account statistics in parallel so the profile card and stat cards appear together.

## Update your profile details

Your profile card shows three fields:

- **Full Name** — editable
- **Email** — read-only
- **Member Since** — read-only

Nomadfiling reads these values from your account profile, including your email address, your saved full name, and your account creation date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile card" icon="users">

  Go to `/account` after signing in. The profile card appears near the top of the page and shows your current account details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your full name" icon="edit">

  Select the pencil icon next to **Full Name** to enter edit mode. Nomadfiling replaces the text with an input field and shows **Save** and **Cancel** actions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or discard the change" icon="check-circle">

  Enter your updated name, then select **Save** to keep it. If you change your mind, select **Cancel** to leave your current name unchanged.

  A successful update returns the profile card to its normal view with the new full name displayed.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

Use your real name if you want your account details to stay consistent across support, billing, and filing activity.

</Callout>

## Understand the fields you see

The account page keeps editable and read-only fields separate so you can quickly see what can change.

<ParamField body="full_name" param-type="string" required="false" show-location="false">
Your display name on the account page. You can update this field inline from the profile card.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" param-type="string" required="true" show-location="false">
The email address tied to your signed-in account. The account page shows this value as read-only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="created_at" param-type="datetime" required="true" show-location="false">
The date your account was created. The account page displays this as **Member Since** and does not allow editing.
</ParamField>

## View your account statistics

The account page includes quick stats so you can confirm recent activity without opening multiple sections.

### Filings count

The filings stat shows how many filings belong to your account. Select the filings card to go to the [Dashboard](/dashboard), where you can review saved filings in more detail.

### Paid payments count

The paid payments stat shows the number of payments on your account with a paid status. Select the payments card to open [Payments](/account/payments).

<Callout kind="info">

The payments count includes paid payments only. Unpaid or incomplete payment records do not increase this number.

</Callout>

## Open MCP API keys

The account page includes a separate card for **MCP API Keys**. Use it to open your API key management page at [API Keys](/account/api-keys).

This shortcut is useful if you work with external tools or automations and need to create, review, or manage account-level keys.

## Sign out safely

Use the logout action in the account page or main navigation when you are done working in Nomadfiling.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Logout" icon="x-circle">

  Click **Logout** from the account page navigation or the mobile navigation menu.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear local session state" icon="trash">

  During logout, Nomadfiling clears the local `tax_tool_paid` browser value before ending your session.

  </Step>
  <Step title="End the authenticated session" icon="lock">

  Nomadfiling signs you out and redirects you to `/`.

  Success looks like a return to the public home page, where account-only pages are no longer accessible until you sign in again.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use account navigation

The signed-in account navigation helps you move between the main areas of Nomadfiling without returning to the home page.

You can access these items from the main navigation:

- **Dashboard**
- **Fax Outbox**
- **Payments**
- **Account**
- **Language** switcher
- **Logout**

On smaller screens, the same navigation is available from the mobile hamburger menu. When you are on the account page, **Account** appears as the active section.

## Password and sign-in access

Your account page focuses on profile details, account activity, and access to related account settings.

If you need help with billing before continuing, review [Pricing](/getting-started/pricing). If you cannot reach the account page because you are not signed in, start from the login screen and complete your sign-in flow first.

<Callout kind="alert">

The account page does not expose every authentication action directly in the profile card. If you are troubleshooting account access, sign in again first and confirm that `/account` loads successfully before changing anything else.

</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why can I edit my full name but not my email?" default-open="true">

  Nomadfiling allows inline editing for your full name only. The email field is shown for reference and remains read-only on the account page.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why does the payments count look lower than expected?">

  The payments card counts only payments with a paid status. Pending, failed, or incomplete payments are not included in that number.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why did Nomadfiling send me to the login page when I opened /account?">

  The account page is protected. If your session has expired or you were not signed in, Nomadfiling redirects you to `/login` before loading account data.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens when I log out?">

  Nomadfiling clears the local `tax_tool_paid` browser value, signs you out, and redirects you to the home page. After that, protected account pages require a new sign-in.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Where do I manage API keys?">

  Open the **MCP API Keys** card on the account page, or go directly to [API Keys](/account/api-keys).

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
