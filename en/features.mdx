## File US tax forms with guided workflows

Nomadfiling helps you prepare, review, and deliver IRS filing documents for foreign-owned LLCs and partnerships from one dashboard. Use guided wizards for core tax forms, generate IRS-ready PDFs, add supporting documents, and send eligible filings by fax without leaving the product.

<Callout kind="info">

Nomadfiling supports foreign-owned single-member US LLC workflows and partnership workflows. Some features, such as direct fax delivery and AI checks, are optional parts of the filing flow rather than separate products.

</Callout>

## Complete Form 5472 and Form 1120 in one wizard

The Form 5472 and Form 1120 workflow is built for foreign-owned single-member US LLCs that need an annual information return package. The wizard walks you from entity details through final PDF generation so you can review every section before download.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter entity and owner details" icon="building2">

  Add reporting corporation information for the LLC, then enter the foreign owner details used in the filing package. This covers the core business identity data needed for Form 5472 and Form 1120.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add related parties and transactions" icon="users">

  Enter one or more related parties, then record the reportable transactions tied to each party. The workflow supports multiple related parties in the same filing session.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign, review, and generate PDFs" icon="check-circle">

  Add a signature, include supporting statements if needed, review the filing, and generate the final PDFs. The last step includes PDF preview and download.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handle extensions and late filings

Not every filing starts with a standard annual return. Nomadfiling also covers two common exception workflows: filing an extension and documenting reasonable cause for a late Form 5472 filing.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Form 7004 extension" icon="clock">

  Use the Form 7004 workflow when you need more time to file. The process includes a 6-question eligibility quiz, form generation, and an optional IRS fax delivery step.

  A good fit for this workflow is a filing that is not ready by the due date but still qualifies for an extension request.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Late filing letter" icon="alert-triangle">

  Use the late filing letter generator when the due date has already passed and you need to submit a reasonable cause statement with your filing package. The generated PDF is designed for late Form 5472 situations and references the Farhy v. Commissioner case.

  This workflow is separate from an extension request. It helps you explain a missed deadline rather than request more time before the deadline.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="alert">

A Form 7004 extension request and a late filing letter solve different problems. Use the extension workflow before the deadline when eligible. Use the late filing letter after a missed deadline to document reasonable cause.

</Callout>

### How the AI features fit into the workflow

The compliance scanner analyzes generated PDFs after the filing data has been assembled. Supporting statement generation helps earlier in the process when you are preparing attachment text for the filing package.

<Callout kind="tip">

Treat AI output as a review layer, not a substitute for checking your filing data. Confirm names, dates, amounts, entity details, and transaction entries before you generate the final PDFs.

</Callout>
