## Complete your first filing

Create your first Form 5472 and Form 1120 filing by moving through Nomadfiling's guided 8-step wizard, paying for your plan, and downloading a ZIP bundle of ready-to-file PDFs.

<Callout kind="info">

You can start from the main filing flow at [app.nomadfiling.com](https://app.nomadfiling.com/) or use the guided quiz flow at [app.nomadfiling.com/quiz](https://app.nomadfiling.com/quiz) if you want more help while entering information.

</Callout>

### Before you start

Gather the details below before you open the wizard. Having them ready makes the filing flow much faster.

- **LLC details** — legal LLC name, business address, EIN, incorporation date, tax year dates, total assets, business activity, and NAICS code
- **Foreign owner details** — full name, address, tax residency, and foreign tax identification number
- **Related party details** — each related party's name, address, relationship to the LLC or owner, and tax residency information
- **Transaction amounts** — amounts received from and paid to each related party for the reporting year
- **Signature details** — signer title, signature image if you plan to upload one, and signature date

<Callout kind="tip">

If you have multiple related parties, prepare each party's transaction amounts separately. Nomadfiling generates one Form 5472 per related party.

</Callout>

### Follow the full workflow

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your account" icon="users">

Go to [app.nomadfiling.com/register](https://app.nomadfiling.com/register) and create your account.

After you sign up, you can return to the filing flow anytime from your account dashboard. When registration succeeds, you are ready to start a new filing.

</Step>

<Step title="Start a new filing" icon="play-circle">

Open the filing wizard from [app.nomadfiling.com](https://app.nomadfiling.com/) or the guided quiz at [app.nomadfiling.com/quiz](https://app.nomadfiling.com/quiz).

Use the main flow if you already know the information you need to enter. Use the quiz flow if you want more guidance before filling out the form.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter entity information" icon="building-2">

Add the reporting corporation details for your foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity.

Include your LLC name, street address, EIN, total assets, business activity, NAICS code, incorporation date, country of incorporation, and tax year. You will also enter business country and tax residency details where requested.

A successful step leaves you with a complete entity profile and tax year range that carries into the generated forms.

</Step>

<Step title="Add foreign owner information" icon="users">

Enter the foreign owner's identifying details exactly as you want them to appear on the filing.

Provide the owner's name, address, tax residency country, and foreign tax identification number. If you have a U.S. ID or reference ID for the owner, add those details as well.

When this step is complete, the owner section of Form 5472 has the information needed for Part II.

</Step>

<Step title="Add related parties" icon="database">

Add each related party connected to the filing.

For every related party, enter the name, address, foreign or domestic status, relationship type, business activity, business code, business countries, and tax resident country. Nomadfiling supports multiple related parties, so repeat this step until every party is listed.

When you finish, the filing contains the parties needed to generate separate Form 5472 documents.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter transaction amounts" icon="bar-chart">

Record the reportable transactions for each related party.

Enter amounts for the Form 5472 Part IV lines that apply to your filing, including amounts the LLC received and amounts the LLC paid. Keep your books or accounting records nearby so the values match your records for the tax year.

Review the totals before moving on. This is the step most likely to cause downstream filing errors if values are incomplete or assigned to the wrong party.

</Step>

<Step title="Add your signature" icon="edit">

Sign the filing by drawing a signature or uploading a signature image.

Add the signature date and signer title on the same step. Nomadfiling places this information on Form 1120, which is included in the final ZIP bundle.

You are done with this step when the preview shows your signature clearly and the signer title is correct.

</Step>

<Step title="Prepare supporting statements" icon="paperclip">

Add any supporting statement text needed for Part V or Part VI and choose optional filing documents.

You can enter custom statement text, include capital contribution or distribution details where relevant, and decide whether to generate supporting statements. Optional documents may also be available depending on your filing needs.

If you do not need extra statements, continue after confirming the step reflects your filing accurately.

</Step>

<Step title="Review everything before payment" icon="check-circle">

Check the read-only review step carefully before you pay.

Confirm names, addresses, EIN, transaction amounts, related party relationships, and signature details. This is your last full review point before PDF generation and checkout.

When the review looks correct, continue to the PDF and checkout step.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a plan and pay" icon="lock">

Select the pricing plan that matches how many filings you need, then complete payment with Stripe.

| Plan | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| $147 | 1 filing |
| $197 | 2 filings |
| $297 | 5 filings |
| $497 | Unlimited filings |

After payment succeeds, Nomadfiling saves the filing to the database and redirects you to your filing page at `app.nomadfiling.com/filing/:id`.

</Step>

<Step title="Preview and download your PDFs" icon="download">

Open the saved filing page to preview and download the generated documents.

Nomadfiling creates a ZIP bundle that can include:

- **Form 5472** for each related party
- **Form 1120**
- **Fax cover sheet** if you chose to generate one
- **Supporting statements** if you added them

A successful download gives you a ZIP file containing the PDFs for that filing. Keep the ZIP in a secure location so you can return to it later.

</Step>

<Step title="Run optional checks and delivery steps" icon="shield">

Use the AI compliance scanner if you want another pass over the generated PDFs before sending them to the IRS.

If you plan to fax the filing, you can also use Nomadfiling's fax service to send the PDF package to the IRS. This is optional, but it can simplify delivery if you do not want to handle faxing yourself.

</Step>

</Steps>

### What you receive after checkout

Your completed filing is saved, and you are redirected to a dedicated filing page where you can preview and download the documents again later.

The final ZIP bundle can include the core forms plus optional extras:

- **One Form 5472 per related party**
- **One Form 1120**
- **Optional fax cover sheet**
- **Optional supporting statements**

<Callout kind="success">

If the redirect succeeds, your filing has been saved. You can return to it later from the dashboard at [app.nomadfiling.com/dashboard](https://app.nomadfiling.com/dashboard).

</Callout>

### Common questions

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="Should I use the main filing flow or the quiz flow?" default-open="true">

Use the main flow at `app.nomadfiling.com/` if you already know what information belongs on the filing.

Use the quiz flow at `app.nomadfiling.com/quiz` if you want more guidance before filling out the forms. Both paths lead into the filing workflow.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Do I need to finish the filing in one session?">

No. After payment, the filing is saved and available at its filing page. You can also return through your dashboard to access saved work tied to your account.

If you are preparing information before checkout, it is still best to gather all required details first so you can review the full filing in one pass.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why are there multiple Form 5472 PDFs in my ZIP file?">

Nomadfiling generates one Form 5472 for each related party in the filing.

If you added three related parties, your ZIP bundle includes three separate Form 5472 PDFs plus the Form 1120 and any optional documents you selected.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if I need a fax cover sheet or supporting statements?">

Add those during the supporting statements and PDF steps.

If you choose those options, Nomadfiling includes them in the final ZIP bundle. If you also use the fax service, the fax-ready documents are available as part of that workflow.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What does the AI compliance scanner do?">

The AI compliance scanner reviews your generated PDFs for potential IRS filing issues before you send them.

Use it as a final quality check, especially if this is your first filing or if the filing includes multiple related parties and transaction lines.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
