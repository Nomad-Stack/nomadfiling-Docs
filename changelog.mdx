## Recent updates

Follow product changes across Nomadfiling, including new filing workflows, delivery options, AI features, and platform improvements. Dates below reflect the rollout sequence visible in the product codebase and migration timeline.

<Callout kind="info">

Release dates are approximate where the codebase does not expose a public launch date. Entries are ordered to reflect the observed product rollout from March through May 2026.

</Callout>

<Update label="2026-05" description="MCP access, API keys, and AI workflow expansion" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Added MCP server access for AI agents

Nomadfiling added an MCP server with 14 tools for AI-assisted workflows. This makes it possible to connect compatible AI clients to filing and compliance workflows through OAuth 2.1 with PKCE.

## Added API key management

You can now create and revoke API keys for MCP access from the product. This gives teams a separate access path for agent-based workflows without relying only on interactive sign-in.

## Expanded AI filing assistance

AI-powered compliance scanning and supporting statement generation were added to reduce manual review work. The compliance scanner analyzes uploaded PDFs, and the statement generator helps draft supporting language for filing attachments.

## Improved product discovery and planning

A feature roadmap with upvoting is now available in the app. This gives you a clearer view of what the team is considering and a direct way to signal demand.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-05" description="Extensions, late filing support, and IRS fax delivery" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Added Form 7004 extension filing support

Nomadfiling introduced a Form 7004 workflow with an eligibility quiz and extension form generation. This helps you determine whether an extension applies before preparing the filing package.

## Added late filing letter generation

You can now generate a late filing letter with a reasonable cause statement as part of the filing workflow. This is designed for cases where you need supporting documentation alongside delinquent filings.

## Added fax delivery through Telnyx

Fax sending was added for IRS delivery workflows, including an outbox and fax status handling. This gives you an in-product option for forms and documents that still rely on fax submission.

<Callout kind="tip">

If your filing requires fax delivery, check the generated package before sending so the page count, cover page details, and attached forms match your submission.

</Callout>

</Update>

<Update label="2026-04" description="Partnership filing support and withholding forms" tags={["feature"]}>

## Added partnership filing workflows

Nomadfiling expanded beyond foreign-owned single-member LLC filings to support partnership filings. The new workflow covers Form 1065 and related partner reporting documents.

## Added Schedule K-1, K-2, and K-3 support

Partnership filings now include partner-level and international reporting outputs, including Schedule K-1, Schedule K-2, and Schedule K-3 data collection. This broadens the platform for partnerships with cross-border tax reporting needs.

## Added Forms 8804, 8805, and 1040-NR support

The partnership release also added withholding and nonresident filing support through Forms 8804, 8805, and 1040-NR. This connects partnership income allocation and withholding workflows in the same filing system.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-03" description="Core platform launch and account foundations" tags={["feature"]}>

## Launched the first filing workflow

Nomadfiling launched with a guided Form 5472 and Form 1120 workflow for foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entities. The core experience includes step-by-step data entry, PDF generation, review, and packaged filing outputs.

## Added account access and payments

The initial release included Supabase Auth for account access and Stripe for one-time payments. This established the core account and checkout flow for paid filing generation.

## Added the dashboard and filing management basics

The first dashboard experience gave users a place to manage filings and move through the preparation flow. The launch also included the one-time pricing model across four tiers, ranging from $147 to $497.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-03" description="Localization, affiliate tracking, and analytics integrations" tags={["improvement", "feature"]}>

## Added 16-language interface support

Nomadfiling introduced multilingual UI support across 16 languages. This makes the filing workflow easier to navigate for users who prefer to work in a language other than English.

## Added affiliate tracking

Affiliate attribution was added through Tolt.io with Stripe-connected referral tracking. This supports partner and referral programs tied to paid conversions.

## Added conversion tracking integrations

Meta Conversions API and Google Ads conversion tracking were added to improve measurement of acquisition and purchase events. These integrations support campaign attribution and reporting across marketing channels.

</Update>

## What changed overall

The platform has moved from a focused Form 5472 and Form 1120 filing tool into a broader tax filing system for foreign-owned LLCs and partnerships. Recent work concentrated on three areas:

- **More filing coverage** through partnership, extension, and late filing workflows
- **More delivery and automation** through fax delivery, AI assistance, MCP access, and API keys
- **More accessibility and operations support** through multilingual UI, affiliate tracking, analytics, and roadmap voting
