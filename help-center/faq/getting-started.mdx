description: Learn how to create your first Nomadfiling project.
---

## Steps

1. Sign up at `https://app.nomadfiling.com`.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Click "New Project" on your dashboard.
4. Enter a project name and description.
5. Click "Create" to save your project.

Your new project is ready to organize documentation!

## Example

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.nomadfiling.com/v1/projects \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"name": "My Project"}'
```
