## Fix the most common login problems

Most login issues come from an incorrect email address, an expired session, a missed password reset email, or a browser problem that interrupts authentication. Work through the steps below in order so you can rule out the fastest fixes first.

<Callout kind="info">

Nomadfiling uses email and password sign-in at `/login`. If your session expired, sign in again with the same account. Your payment status is stored separately in your browser, so logging in again does not remove it.

</Callout>

## Try these fixes in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the email address you entered" icon="check-circle">

Verify that you are using the same email address you used to register your account at `/register`. Look for common mistakes such as missing characters, extra spaces, or the wrong email domain.

If you see an `Invalid credentials` message, the email address or password does not match an existing account. Confirm the email first before you reset the password.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password" icon="lock">

Open `/forgot-password`, enter your email address, and submit the form. Nomadfiling sends a password reset email that links you to `/reset-password`, where you can set a new password.

After you save the new password, return to `/login` and sign in again. If the reset worked, you should reach your account instead of returning to the login screen.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your spam or junk folder" icon="mail">

Password reset emails can be filtered by your email provider. Search your inbox, spam, junk, and promotions folders for messages related to Nomadfiling or password reset instructions.

If you do not find the email, go back to `/forgot-password`, confirm the email address, and request a new reset link. Wait a few minutes before trying again.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache and cookies for app.nomadfiling.com" icon="trash">

Clear your browser's saved site data for `app.nomadfiling.com`, then open the app again and sign in from `/login`. This removes stale session data that can cause the login page to reload without showing an error.

If your browser lets you clear data for a single site, remove only the data for `app.nomadfiling.com`. That is usually faster than clearing all browsing data.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Try an incognito or private browsing window" icon="monitor">

Open a private browsing window and go directly to `app.nomadfiling.com`. Then try signing in with the same email and password.

If login works in private browsing, the issue is usually cached data, a browser extension, or another browser setting in your normal profile. Continue with the next step to narrow it down.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable browser extensions" icon="minus">

Turn off ad blockers, privacy tools, script blockers, and similar extensions temporarily. Some extensions interfere with the authentication flow and can cause the page to refresh instead of completing sign-in.

After disabling extensions, reload `app.nomadfiling.com` and try again. If login succeeds, re-enable extensions one at a time to find the one causing the problem.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check whether the app is reachable" icon="cloud">

Visit `app.nomadfiling.com` directly in your browser. If the app does not load at all, the problem may be your network connection, VPN, firewall, or a temporary connectivity issue.

Try another network if possible, or disconnect from a VPN and reload the page. Once the app loads normally, try signing in again.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different supported browser" icon="external-link">

Sign in using another browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. This helps confirm whether the problem is limited to one browser profile or browser configuration.

If login works in another browser, return to your original browser and clear site data, disable extensions, or update the browser to the latest version.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Match the symptom to the likely fix

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="I see Invalid credentials" default-open="true">

This message usually means the email address or password is wrong. Start by checking the email address carefully, then use `/forgot-password` to set a new password if needed.

If the reset email never arrives, check spam or junk folders and submit the reset request again using the exact email address you used to sign up.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="The login page reloads but nothing happens">

This pattern usually points to stale browser data or an extension interfering with the sign-in flow. Clear site data for `app.nomadfiling.com`, then try again in an incognito or private window.

If the problem only happens in one browser, disable extensions and test another supported browser.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="I did not receive the password reset email">

Check spam, junk, promotions, and filtered folders first. Then return to `/forgot-password`, confirm the email address, and request another reset link.

If you still do not receive the email after a few minutes, try from another browser and make sure `app.nomadfiling.com` is loading correctly.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="I was logged in before, but now I am signed out">

Your session may have expired. Go back to `/login` and sign in again with your email and password.

Nomadfiling uses session-based authentication, so being signed out does not usually mean your account or payment status was removed.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## When to contact support

If you worked through all of the steps above and still cannot access your account, contact support with the details that will help reproduce the issue quickly.

<Callout kind="alert">

Include the email address tied to your account, the exact error message you see, the browser and device you used, and which troubleshooting steps you already tried.

</Callout>
