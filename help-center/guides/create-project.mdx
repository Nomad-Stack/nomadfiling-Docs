## Create your first project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Nomadfiling">
    Visit `https://app.nomadfiling.com` and enter your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    Click "New Project" at the top right of your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    Enter your project's name and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the project">
    Click "Create". Your project will appear in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can now add documentation and invite collaborators.
