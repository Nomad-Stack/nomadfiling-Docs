## Before you troubleshoot

<Callout kind="tip">

Have these details ready before you open a help article:
- Your LLC and owner information
- The filing step where you got stuck
- Whether the issue affects login, data entry, review, or PDF generation
- Any wording shown in the app when the problem occurred

</Callout>

That context makes it easier to choose the right guide and confirm whether the issue is related to setup, filing data, or document generation.
